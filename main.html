Ours is one I think about more than I’d admit. The way things went from casual to comfortable — from just talking to genuinely looking forward to seeing you. It didn’t announce itself. It just grew, quietly, the way the best things tend to.

Before I knew it, you were already my favourite part of my day. Not just sometimes — consistently. And I think that’s when I knew this was different.

“I love how you make any moment feel easy.”

I love that your laugh takes over a room and you genuinely don’t care. I love how hardworking you are — the way you hustle, push yourself, show up for your goals without making a big deal of it. I see that. I respect you so much for it.

I love that a conversation with you turns into an entire night without warning, and that I’d let it happen every single time. Just having you around — talking, laughing, teasing each other — feels more like home than anything else.

“I want those little moments to become our everyday life.”

I want to celebrate more of your birthdays, more of your wins, more of your ordinary days that somehow feel extraordinary because you’re in them. I want to see you graduate with that First Class, watch your content creation take off, and be the person cheering loudest in the crowd.

However the future looks — I know one thing. It’s better with you in it. Here’s to everything that comes next. ❤️
